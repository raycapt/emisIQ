Fuel Properties Reference
How fuel values differ by regulation — and why FuelEU has no CF (tCO₂/t) column: CII / EEXI / EEDI / EU ETS use the direct combustion CO₂ factor (CF) in tCO₂/t fuel — tank-to-wake only, per MARPOL Annex VI Table 1. Upstream emissions are invisible to these frameworks. FuelEU Maritime & IMO NZF/GFI operate entirely in gCO₂eq/MJ Well-to-Wake — there is no CF in tCO₂/t under FuelEU. The WtW column IS the FuelEU metric. It covers all GHGs (CO₂ + CH₄ + N₂O as CO₂eq) across the full supply chain per unit of energy. The TTW column (gCO₂/MJ, combustion only) is the bridge between the two: TTW × LCV ÷ 1000 = CF in tCO₂/t — you can verify: VLSFO 76.9 × 41.0 ÷ 1000 = 3.151 ✓. The WtW–TTW gap is the upstream penalty — largest for grey H₂ and fossil LNG (methane slip).
